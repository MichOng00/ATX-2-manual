Who should use this handbook?

  • Students and other people who are interested in applying microcontrollers to test the working process of automatic systems, or who want to learn and examine microcontrollers in new ways, such as using an autonomous robot as a form of interactive media.
  • Academic institutes such as schools, colleges and universities that provide electronics subjects or electronic and computing engineering departments.
  • Lecturers and teachers who would like to study and prepare lesson plans for microcontroller courses, including applied science that integrates electronics, microcontrollers, computer programming and scientific examination in high school, vocational and bachelor’s degree education.

Clarification from the writer and compiler team

All illustrations and technical information in this handbook are intended to simplify working processes and equipment principles so that they are easy to understand for anyone interested in robotics.

The translation from Thai to English, and the use of technical terms, may not follow the provisions of the Royal Academy, where many words are not described officially. The team has allowed itself to produce new technical terms.

The main reason for this explanation is the data collection on equipment in embedded computer systems and robotic technology. Thai is quite hard to translate into English, so the writer team gathered the required data and investigated to keep errors in the description of working processes to a minimum.

When the information was composed in Thai, many technical terms had complicated meanings, and the definitions came from practice coordinated with linguistic meaning. If errors or mistakes are shown, the team accepts them, and will clarify and improve them as soon as possible if experts give explanations or suggestions.

To develop academic media, especially with new technological knowledge, work can proceed continually with the participation of experts in all fields.
